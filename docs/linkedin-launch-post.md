Dinner is over.
The bill-splitting admin isn't.

One person pays. Then come the calculations, the screenshots, and the “I'll send it later” messages.

I built SplitUPI to explore a simpler moment at the table: one bill, clear shares, UPI requests that all point to the same merchant.

Here's the flow:

→ Enter the merchant and bill total.
→ Split equally, choose custom amounts, or use a fixed amount per payment.
→ Open each UPI request or scan its QR in your payment app.

The total stays exact, down to the last paise. Everything starts in your phone's browser, with no account or app download required for SplitUPI.

For example: a ₹4,800 dinner becomes four clear ₹1,200 requests to the restaurant, if the restaurant accepts separate payments.

It's an early prototype. Confirmations are manual; the merchant still needs to verify the money arrived.

The design question I'm testing: can making the split clear before anyone pays make the end of dinner a little easier?

Try the demo: https://splitupi.webnaut.in

When you eat out with friends, do you prefer everyone paying the restaurant directly, or one person paying and settling later?
